## What the brief asks for
A **neuro-symbolic knowledge-based intelligent system in Python**: a trained/fine-tuned deep-learning model whose predictions become symbolic facts, reasoned over by a hand-built knowledge base (**20–25 rules**) + inference engine (forward/backward chaining, unification) with reasoning traces — implementing a **2026 research paper** from an impact-factor IEEE/Elsevier/Springer journal.

**Marks:** 100 (paper 10 · DL 15 · KR/KB 20 · rules+inference 20 · integration 15 · queries+explainability 10 · testing/demo 10).

## Lessons worth keeping
- **When two official briefs conflict, the later one governs only once the owner confirms it** — and the superseded brief stays on file, labelled, not deleted.
- **A repo scoped to one project stays scoped to one project** — references to sibling candidates don't belong in it, even as "related work".
- **Paper-first gating:** no implementation before the paper + domain are approved; the shortlist is ranked by dataset availability first (public dataset beats a higher impact factor with an unfindable dataset).
