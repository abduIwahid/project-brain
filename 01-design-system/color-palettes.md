first — Classic, Playful, Organic, Minimalist — then the exact shades.

ones; palettes chosen per context, not always colourful; defined colour

The 60-30-10 proportion rule (via saved-references item 118, @uiuxaadi):
when laying a palette over a design, ~60% is the dominant colour (usually
the neutral/background), ~30% the secondary/primary, and ~10% the accent —
the accent stays rare so it keeps its punch. Use as a sanity check on any
new palette or page: if the accent covers much more than a tenth of the
screen, the hierarchy is probably off. Abdul Wahid's shipped systems already
follow it (MediSight 2A; portfolio black/white + orange).

CMYK print basics (via saved-references item 140, @gfx.om): screens are
RGB (light, bright); print is CMYK (ink, subtractive, slightly muted) —
Cyan/Magenta/Yellow/Key-black. Anything destined for paper (documents,
posters, cards) must be built/exported in CMYK at 300 DPI, and brand
colours (his portfolio orange, MediSight blues) will print a touch duller
than they look on screen — proof before a big print run.

Colour psychology — meaning first, colour second (via saved-references
item 154, @gfx.om "Color Psychology & Pairing", image-verified):
Red = energy/passion/urgency · Orange = creativity/enthusiasm/
friendliness · Yellow = optimism/happiness/clarity · Green = health/
balance/growth · Blue = trust/calm/professionalism · Purple = luxury/
creativity/wisdom · Pink = love/compassion/playfulness · Black =
elegance/power/sophistication · White = purity/simplicity/clarity ·
Gray = neutrality/balance/modernity · Beige = warmth/simplicity/
natural · Teal = freshness/clarity/confidence · Navy = stability/
intelligence/trust · Coral = energy/warmth/friendliness · Mint =
freshness/calm/renewal · Brown = reliability/comfort/authenticity.
Pairing notes that matter for his systems: Navy pairs White/Gray/Beige/
Gold; Blue pairs White/Gray/Orange/Yellow; cool colours (blue/green/
purple) read calm + trustworthy, warm colours read energetic. His three
locked palettes all match the sheet: WhyRisk navy+steel-blue (trust/
calm/stability — the healthcare-correct read), Study Hub deep green
(growth), portfolio black+orange (power + creativity). Use this map when
a NEW surface needs a colour decision: pick the feeling first, then the
colour that carries it (same doctrine as item 132's logo fonts).
