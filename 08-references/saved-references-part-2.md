125. **Border Radius Tips — the nested-corner rule** (creator unavailable logged-out; slide-verified, content fully legible) — https://www.instagram.com/p/DdzHhSLDnnu/
   25 Oct 2026. A single precise UI rule, shown as a wrong/right pair: when one rounded element sits inside another with padding, do NOT copy the outer radius to the inner element (outer 24px + inner 24px is marked wrong — the inner corner bulges because the arcs don't share a centre). The correct relation is **outer R = inner R + padding** (with 8px padding: outer 24px = inner 16px + 8px), which keeps both arcs on the same centre point and the gap between the curves perfectly even. The slide's footer adds: alternatively apply a multiplier, and to test, scale the inner frame up until it matches the outer frame's size. Relevance for Abdul Wahid: a daily-use rule for his card-heavy UIs (cards inside cards, images inside rounded cards, modal contents) across Study Hub, the portfolio and MediSight — distilled as a hard rule in brain/general/frontend-preferences.md.
