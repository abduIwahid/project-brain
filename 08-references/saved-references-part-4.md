261. **"Don't ship your UI before designing these 10 states" (@strategyfox; caption + full video verified)** — https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfHuIGABu_B/
   Filed 4 Nov 2026. A design-review checklist: a product is tested by what it does when things go wrong, not on the happy path. The 10 states, each with one rule: (1) Empty — say why it's empty + one next step. (2) Loading — show the shape of what's coming (skeleton), not a spinner. (3) Error — what went wrong, whose fault, how to fix it. (4) No internet — keep cached content visible, say what still works. (5) Slow network — real progress + a lighter path (lite mode). (6) No search results — fix the typo for them, suggest the next search. (7) Permission denied — explain why you need it + a way back. (8) Session expired — save their work before signing them out. (9) Form validation — flag the exact field, inline, with the fix. (10) Success — confirm, reassure, then show what happens next. Relevance to Abdul Wahid: a ready pre-ship checklist for every project — WhyRisk (its error/empty/loading states are Phase-7-grade polish), Study Hub (search no-results + download states), Portfolio (contact-form validation/success; his 404 is already a designed state), and the MTM hackathon build (10 states = cheap judge-visible completeness). Distilled into the brain's frontend preferences as a standing states checklist.
