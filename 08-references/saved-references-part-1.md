## 5 Hero Styles to Try Instead — @ose.builds (Instagram reel)

- **Saved:** 2026-10-23 (Abdul Wahid: "save for future projects")
- **Link:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DezxScEuE0I/
- **Premise:** Most sites use the same hero — headline + button on the left, product image on the right. It works, but nobody remembers it. Pick the style that fits your industry instead.

1. **Split** — your headline breaks around your product. Best for: fintech, SaaS, single-product brands.
2. **Full-Bleed** — giant type over one edge-to-edge photo. Best for: hardware and physical products.
3. **Orbit** — headline in the middle, photos floating around it. Best for: agencies, salons, clinics, real estate.
4. **Gallery** — a wall of your work framing the headline. Best for: AI tools and creative studios.
5. **Wordmark** — your brand name huge, product layered on top. Best for: fashion, events, creator brands.

**Relevance to Abdul Wahid's work:** his portfolio hero (Oct 2026) is closest to Wordmark/Split with the portrait overlapping the headline. Gallery fits an AI-tool landing page (e.g. a MediSight AI or Study Hub landing); Orbit suits agency/clinic-style pages.
