# Animation & motion vocabulary

Distilled from filed reels #277 (@sohail_systems, 16 types), #280 (@bendoescareer, 19 UX/animation principles) and #279 (@crownzdesigns, 10 scroll effects). Full entries: `../08-references/`.

## The 16 animation types
Hover · Stagger · 3D Flip · Magnetic · Accordion · Morphing · Gradient · Text Reveal · Image Reveal · Parallax · Marquee · Menu Transition · Toggle · Success Message · Horizontal Scroll Transition · Lenis (smooth scroll).

**Strong candidates in house style:** Magnetic buttons, Stagger (refines ordinary reveals), Success Message animation (peak-end rule — form completions, downloads).
**Cautions:** Lenis is trial-only (dependency weight vs. benefit). Horizontal Scroll Transition is scroll-driven — a class repeatedly rejected on the owner's portfolio; vocabulary only there.

## Motion principles (the ones that change decisions)
- **Doherty threshold** — respond within ~400ms. **Timing** — UI animations under ~300ms.
- **Slow in / slow out, Arcs, Follow-through, Secondary action, Anticipation, Squash & stretch, Exaggeration, Rubber banding** — physics-feeling motion beats motor-like linear motion: momentum, overshoot, body lag, gentle sway.
- **Interruptible motion** — the user can always take over mid-animation.
- **Origin awareness** — elements animate from where they were triggered.
- **Staggering** — lists enter as a cascade, not a block.
- **Peak-end rule** — the end of a flow deserves design effort (success states).

## The 10 scroll effects (vocabulary)
Layered Parallax · Pinned Heading · 3D Card Zoom · Line-by-line Reveal · Sideways Text · Card Arc Carousel · Fixed 3D Model · Stacking Cards · Focus-frame Slider · Scroll-triggered Demo.

Shipped and proven on the owner's portfolio: **3D Card Zoom** + **Line-by-line Reveal** (transform-only, one-shot). **Stacking Cards** is the owner's chosen pattern for project lists. The rest are filed as vocabulary — most are scroll-driven pinned flows, which the owner has twice rejected on the portfolio in favour of stacking cards.
